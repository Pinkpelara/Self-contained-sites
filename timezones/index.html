How to read the table

Green rows are inside the work day.

In hours means 9 to 5

A city counts as in hours from 9:00 in the morning up to but not including 5:00 in the afternoon local time. Aim for a time with the most green rows.

Offsets use UTC labels

Each row shows a UTC offset like UTC-05:00 or UTC+05:30 for that exact date, so daylight saving shifts show up without any extra step from you.

Questions people actually ask

How is daylight saving handled?

Through your browser built-in zone data. A March date in New York reads UTC-05:00 before the spring shift and UTC-04:00 after it, with no table to update.

Why do half hour zones matter?

Places like Mumbai sit at UTC+05:30, so a round UTC hour lands on the half hour there. The table shows the exact local minutes.

What if my city is missing?

The list covers 24 large cities. For a missing place, pick the nearest city sharing its zone, then confirm against an official clock.

Does the share link track me?

No. The picks sit in the link itself. This page stores nothing and uploads nothing.
