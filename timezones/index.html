Time zone questions

How is daylight saving set?

Your browser sets it. A March date in New York shows UTC-05:00 before the spring shift and UTC-04:00 after it. You update nothing.

Why do half hour zones matter?

Some places like Mumbai sit at UTC+05:30. A full hour elsewhere lands on the half hour there. The table shows exact local minutes.

What if my city is missing?

The list covers 24 large cities. For a missing place, pick the near city in the same zone. Then check with an official clock.

Does the share link track me?

No. Your picks sit in the link itself. This page saves nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
