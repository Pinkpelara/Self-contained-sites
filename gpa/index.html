US 4.0 and 4.3 GPA scales

The 4.0 table runs from A+ at 4.0 down to F at 0.0 with plus and minus steps. The 4.3 choice counts A+ as 4.3 and is the same as 4.0 for the rest. Many US schools cap at 4.0.

India 10 point GPA scale

This page uses O 10, A+ 9, A 8, B+ 7, B 6, C 5, P 4, F 0. Schools set their own bands, so check the pass grade and F cut off for your course.

ECTS guide and percent grades

ECTS has no official 4.0 table. The rough guide here uses A 4.0, B 3.0, C 2.0, D 1.0, E 0.7, F 0. The percent lookup counts 93 and up as 4.0, 90 as 3.7, down to 65 as 1.0.

GPA questions and answers

Is the ECTS grade map official?

No. Use it as a rough guide only. Ask your school which table it uses for transfer credit.

What does retake replace do?

With replace on, only your best try in each course counts. With it off, each try counts. Schools often cap retakes or take the average instead.

Why does one course move my GPA so much?

Credits set the weight. A 4 credit course counts twice as much as a 2 credit course with the same grade.

What if my GPA goal looks too high?

The planner shows the exact average you need. It will warn you if it is past the top of the scale. Talk to a school advisor about retakes, course load, and school rules.
