Which scale fits your school

Pick the table your school uses.

US 4.0 and 4.3

The 4.0 table runs A+ 4.0 down to F 0.0 with plus and minus steps. The 4.3 variant counts A+ as 4.3 and matches 4.0 elsewhere. Many US transcripts cap at 4.0.

India 10 point

This page maps O 10, A+ 9, A 8, B+ 7, B 6, C 5, P 4, F 0. Boards and universities set their own bands, so confirm the passing grade and the F cutoff for your course.

ECTS guide and percent

ECTS publishes no official 4.0 table. The guide here maps A 4.0, B 3.0, C 2.0, D 1.0, E 0.7, F 0. The percent lookup counts 93 and up as 4.0, 90 as 3.7, down to 65 as 1.0.

Questions people actually ask

Is the ECTS mapping official?

No. Use it as a rough guide only and ask your school which table it applies to transfer credit.

What does repeated course replace do?

With replace on, only the best attempt per course code counts toward the term. Without it, every attempt counts. Schools often cap repeats or average attempts instead.

Why does one course move my GPA so much?

Credits weight the math. A 4 credit course counts twice as much as a 2 credit course at the same grade.

What if my target looks out of reach?

The planner shows the exact average needed and flags when it sits above the scale max. Talk to an advisor about retakes, course load, and school policy.
