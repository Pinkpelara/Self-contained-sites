What this page counts

Four counts from one start date.

Due date and trimesters

Due date adds 280 days to the last period date. First trimester runs weeks 1 to 13, second weeks 14 to 27, third week 28 on. The page converts those cutoffs to dates.

Week count

Pick any date to see weeks and days since the last period, plus the week number. The count starts at week 1 on the last period date itself.

Cycle dates

With a cycle length, ovulation counts as cycle minus 14 days from the last period date. The fertile span runs five days before through one day after that day. Next period adds one full cycle.

Questions people actually ask

How is the due date worked out?

By adding 280 days to the first day of the last period. Real due dates move with scan results and personal history, so treat this as a starting estimate.

What if my cycle is not 28 days?

Enter your usual length from 15 to 60 days. The fertile window and next period shift with it. The due date stays at last period plus 280 days, which assumes a standard count.

Do leap years change anything?

No extra step is needed. The math counts exact calendar days, so Feb 29 start dates land correctly.

Can this tell me something is wrong?

No. This page cannot assess health, risk, or symptoms. Contact a qualified professional or local care line with any concern.
