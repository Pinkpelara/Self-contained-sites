What you get

Four results from one start date.

Due date and trimesters

Your due date is about 40 weeks after the first day of your last period. That is 280 days. Weeks 1 to 13 are the first trimester. Weeks 14 to 27 are the second. Week 28 on is the third.

Week count

Pick any date to see how many weeks and days it is since your last period. Week 1 starts on your last period date.

Fertile window and next period

The fertile window means the days when pregnancy is more likely. We count it as five days before through one day after ovulation. Your next period is one full cycle after your last period.

Pregnancy due date questions

How is my due date figured out?

We add 280 days to the first day of your last period. That is about 40 weeks. Real due dates can shift with scan results, so use this as a start.

What if my cycle is not 28 days?

Type your usual length from 15 to 60 days. Your fertile window and next period shift with it. Your due date stays at last period plus 280 days.

Do leap years change anything?

No. The math counts exact calendar days. A start date of Feb 29 still lands right.

Can this tool say if something is wrong?

No. It does calendar math only. It cannot check your health. Call your doctor or a local care line with any worry.
