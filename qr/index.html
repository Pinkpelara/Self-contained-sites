What each type stores

Plain text in, squares out.

Links, email, phone, text

Links must start with http:// or https://. Email makes a mailto link, phone makes a tel link, and text makes an sms link with an optional note. What you type is what the camera reads.

Wi-Fi logins

Enter the exact network name and pick WPA, WEP, or no password. Phones that support Wi-Fi codes will offer to join after scanning. Names and passwords keep their punctuation with a backslash escape.

Contact cards

Contacts use the vCard 3.0 shape with a name plus optional phone, email, and group. Most phones offer to save the contact after scanning. Test on your own phone before you print.

Questions people actually ask

Can I change the link after I print the code?

No. These are static codes with the text baked into the pattern. To point somewhere else, make and print a new code.

Does my text leave my computer?

No. The pattern is drawn on your device. This page sends nothing anywhere.

Why does a long link make a dense code?

More characters need a larger version number and more modules. Short links print larger modules that scan from farther away.

How do I test a code?

Point your phone camera at the screen before you print, then scan the print too. Check Wi-Fi codes on more than one phone when you can.
