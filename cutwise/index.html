Free board calculator for straight cuts

Get more from every board.

Type in your cut list and Cutwise spreads the parts across boards, with your blade width counted. This one runs start to finish on your own computer. Nothing uploads, there is no account, and your numbers stay in the room with you.

Plan your cuts

Built for same-size boards and straight cuts. It does not nest sheet goods.

Your lumber and cut list

One length per line. Add a quantity with x, like 24 x 4. Use decimals such as 24.5, not fractions. All entries use the unit you picked.

Your cut plan

Bars match the real lengths. The blue ticks between parts are the wood your blade turns into sawdust.

Before you make the first cut

Count the blade, check the stock, then cut.

Measure the kerf you actually cut

Kerf is the slot your blade cuts out, often around 1/8 inch for a table saw. Cutwise holds one kerf between neighboring parts. The leftover already has those kerfs taken out, so if the leftover is thinner than your blade, buy a little extra stock.

Enter the board you really have

Trim the ragged ends first, then enter the usable length. This tool assumes every board matches that length. It ignores knots, bow, grain, and structural ratings, so look the lumber over before you commit.

Know what the layout is

Cutwise sorts parts longest-first and fills each board before opening the next. That finds a solid shop layout fast, but not a proven minimum. Double-check every number at the saw.

Questions from the shop

How is saw kerf counted?

One blade width is held between neighboring cuts on the same board, and the leftover shown already has those kerfs removed. Measure the leftover before trusting a final separating cut.

Is this the smallest possible board count?

No. Cutwise uses a longest-first heuristic that finds a practical layout fast. It is a planning aid, not a proven optimum. Verify every measurement before cutting.

Can I enter fractions like 24 1/2?

Not yet. Enter decimals instead, such as 24.5. Use one length per line, with an optional quantity like 24.5 x 4.

How do I save the plan as a PDF?

Press Print plan and choose Save as PDF in your browser print dialog.
