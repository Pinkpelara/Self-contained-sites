What SizeReady does to a photo

Smaller file, same photo, clear tradeoffs.

Everything becomes a JPEG

PNG and WebP inputs are converted. Clear areas turn white, animations become one still frame, and some fine detail goes away. That is the price of a much smaller file.

Your dimensions are ceilings

Photos are never cropped and never enlarged. A small photo stays small. SizeReady first lowers JPEG quality between 0.30 and 0.94, then shrinks dimensions in 20% steps only if you allowed it.

A size fit is not an approval

Job boards, schools, and immigration portals also check format, pixel size, DPI, aspect ratio, or filenames. SizeReady handles the byte count. You still need to check their checklist and preview the file.

Questions people actually ask

What are the limits?

Up to 20 files per batch, 20 MB per file, and 100 MB for the whole batch. No single image may decode beyond 24 megapixels, for example 6,000 by 4,000. File sizes are checked before processing; pixel dimensions are checked after decoding. Decoding itself can use significant memory.

Does KB mean 1,000 or 1,024 bytes?

Here 1 KB is 1,000 bytes, so a 200 KB target means 200,000 bytes or less. That also fits any form that quietly means 1,024 bytes per KB, because 200,000 is smaller than 204,800. If a rule says 250 KiB, just enter 250.

Why did my photo come back smaller in pixels?

Width and height are ceilings, not orders. Photos are never cropped and never enlarged. Your ceilings apply first. If lowering quality is not enough, the checkbox allows shrinking in 20% steps. This is a fast practical search, not a guarantee of the largest possible result.

Can I download everything at once?

No. Each fitted image has its own download link and there is no archive option. Save the files one by one.

Is an under-limit file sure to be accepted?

No. The byte budget is the only promise. Portals can reject files for format, pixel size, aspect ratio, DPI, or naming reasons no size tool can fix. Check the official rules before you submit.

What about transparency or animation?

Clear areas become solid white, and animated inputs become one still frame. Look at the download before you rely on either feature.
