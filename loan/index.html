What the rate means

Reducing balance, flat rate, and APR in plain words.

Reducing balance

This page charges monthly interest on the balance left, so early payments are mostly interest and later ones mostly principal. EMI means the same thing: one fixed monthly figure on a reducing balance.

Flat rate

A flat rate charges the full starting amount for the whole term: amount times rate times years. The comparison row below shows how much more that costs than the same percent on a reducing balance.

APR in one line

APR folds fees and timing into one yearly percent so loans compare fairly. This page prices rate only, with no fees, so treat the result as a base figure before fees.

Questions people actually ask

Is EMI the same as monthly payment?

Yes. Both use payment = P times r over 1 minus (1 plus r) to the minus n, with r the monthly rate and n the month count.

Why is the first year mostly interest?

Interest each month equals the balance times the monthly rate. With a large starting balance, that interest takes most of the fixed payment until the balance falls.

Do extra payments always help?

In this model they cut principal directly, which shortens the term and total interest. Real loans may add prepayment fees or rules, so check the contract.

What is missing from these numbers?

Fees, taxes, insurance, variable rates, and missed payment charges. This page prices principal and rate only.
