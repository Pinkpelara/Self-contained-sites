What the rate means

How the rate works, in plain words.

Reducing balance

This page figures interest each month on what you still owe. Early payments go mostly to interest. Later payments go mostly to what you borrowed. EMI means the same thing: one fixed monthly payment.

Flat rate

A flat rate charges interest on the full starting amount for the whole term. That is amount times rate times years. The comparison row shows how much more that costs than the same percent on a reducing balance.

APR in one line

APR rolls fees and timing into one yearly percent so loans compare fairly. This page counts rate only, with no fees. Treat the result as a starting point before fees.

Common questions

Is EMI the same as monthly payment?

Yes. EMI means a fixed monthly payment. It uses the same math as the monthly payment on this page.

Why is the first year mostly interest?

Each month, interest equals what you still owe times the monthly rate. At the start the balance is large, so interest takes most of the payment. As the balance falls, more of the payment lowers what you borrowed.

Do extra payments always help?

On this page, extra money lowers what you owe right away. That shortens the loan and cuts total interest. Real loans may add early payoff fees or rules. Check your contract.

What is missing from these numbers?

Fees, taxes, insurance, changing rates, and late charges. This page counts the loan amount and the rate only.
