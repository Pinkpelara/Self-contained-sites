Free browser tools

Seven free tools that run in your browser. No sign up. No uploads.

  • Lumber cuts: plan cuts and waste
  • Photo size: shrink photos to fit limits
  • QR codes: make codes for links and Wi-Fi
  • GPA: find semester GPA and CGPA
  • Due dates: due date and trimester dates
  • Meeting times: find times across cities
  • Loan: monthly payment, EMI, and payoff plan
